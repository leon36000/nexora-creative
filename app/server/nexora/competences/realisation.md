Shot rules (Nexora house rules, apply to every storyboard):
- One dominant action per shot; a 5 s shot holds one clear gesture, a 10 s shot one continuous action.
- Open with an establishing or product hero shot, end on the strongest image (the call to action card follows it).
- Vary framing from shot to shot (wide, medium, close-up, detail, low angle) and give each shot one camera move at most.
- Everything that must stay identical (a person's face, hair, clothes; a product's shape and colours; a place's light) is written once in the bible and repeated word for word in each shot description.
- Never ask the video model to draw text, prices, logos, signs or subtitles: leave surfaces blank; text is laid over in post.
- Describe sound sources in the shot (sizzling, door chime, crowd murmur) so the native audio matches the picture.
- Vertical (9:16) shots: keep the subject centred and large; avoid wide landscapes with tiny subjects.
