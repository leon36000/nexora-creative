Nexora AI

Nexora AI: the company and the brand. Written this way wherever the name stands for the company.

Products keep their own names: Nexora WorkStudio, Nexora Code, Nexora RAG… We never write “Nexora AI WorkStudio”.

Never “Nexora Ai”, never “NEXORA AI” (all-caps labels belong to the mono typeface only).

Nexora AI · Brand kit02 · Logo

An “N” in pixels.

Two ink uprights and a diagonal of three orange squares that joins them. The diagonal is the point of passage: what comes in, what is approved, what is controlled. Pixels are the building unit of the whole brand. No robot, no brain.

Nexora AI · Brand kit03 · Construction

Construction.

A 64-unit grid; the ink spans 6 to 58.

Five columns and five rows of 10.4 units, no gaps. Two solid uprights (columns 1 and 5); a diagonal of cells (2, 2), (3, 3) and (4, 4), touching at their corners.

Wordmark: “Nexora” and “A” as outlines of Geist 500 (−0.03 em); the “I” is drawn with serifs so it does not read as “Al”.

Clear space

A margin of at least one upright’s width (10 units out of 64, or 0.2 times the symbol height) on all four sides. No text, page edge or image enters it.

Nexora AI · Brand kit11 · The mosaic

The mosaic.

A field of squares whose hue follows the ember ramp and that dissolves into pixels toward one edge, like an image being computed. Generated in code with a fixed seed: the same image at every render. Text never sits on it.

The visual world: the ember.

Québec night, matter made of light, pixels becoming worlds. Signal orange against deep black and midnight blue. Cinematic, never “stock”. One idea per image, one focal point.
